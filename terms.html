Purpose and access

AI Channel Autopilot is a personal desktop tool used by the owner of Signal & Sense to prepare educational videos and, after configuration, upload and schedule them on an authorised YouTube channel. This website describes the application; it does not provide a public software subscription, hosted generation service or visitor account.

Use is limited to the operator and any people explicitly authorised by the operator. Access to a YouTube channel requires permission from the person entitled to manage it.
